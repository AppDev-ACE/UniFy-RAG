Answer:
Under the Accelerated Credit Registration System, students with no history of arrears and a minimum SGPA and CGPA of 7.5 may register for one additional course per semester, up to three courses in total, from the 4th to 7th semester. Unlike Honours, failing an ACRS course in the first appearance creates a history of arrears and therefore makes you ineligible for First Class with Distinction.
