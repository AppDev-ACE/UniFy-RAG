# UniFy product capabilities

Source: capabilities supplied by the UniFy team/product owner on 2026-07-28.

This source is intentionally separate from SASTRA policy sources. It supports
only the following product-discovery statements:

- UniFy includes a Campus Map.
- Students can view academic-related information in UniFy and in SWI after
  receiving their register number and password.
- UniFy includes Resource Bot and Know Your Professor, which lets students view
  faculty details.
- Hostellers can view the Mess Menu in UniFy.
- The Community Page lets students post queries and seek help from seniors.
- Students can view their timetable in UniFy.
- UniFy has preparation material/features for Placement and GATE exams, plus a
  Career Roadmap.
- Club Hub contains the latest club updates and events.

Do not use this source to make claims about SASTRA regulations, staff decisions,
or a student's personal academic data.
